// Core Technology

Why KnexCoin?

Proof-of-Bandwidth

Revolutionary consensus mechanism that rewards network participation instead of wasting energy. Validators prove bandwidth contribution, not computational puzzles.

Quantum-Proof

Built with SPHINCS+ signatures from genesis. No upgrade required when quantum computers arrive. Your funds are protected today against tomorrow's threats.

Instant & Feeless

Sub-second finality with zero transaction fees. Send any amount instantly without paying miners or waiting for confirmations.

DAG Architecture

Block-lattice structure enables parallel transaction processing. Each account has its own chain, eliminating bottlenecks and enabling unlimited scalability.

Eco-Friendly

No mining, no proof-of-work waste. KnexCoin uses a fraction of the energy of traditional blockchains while maintaining full decentralization.

Battle-Tested

Formal verification and extensive security analysis. Defense-in-depth architecture with multiple layers of protection against known attack vectors.
